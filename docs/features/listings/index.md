Map of `docs/features/listings/`.

- [listing-detail.md](listing-detail.md) — The public page one listing has of its own: what it shows, who may open it, and what each lifecycle state offers there. Read it when working on the buyer-facing listing page or the purchase panel.
- [listing-form.md](listing-form.md) — The page a trader composes a listing on, new or existing: its sections, who may open it, and what the actions say in each state. Read it when working on listing authoring.
- [my-listings.md](my-listings.md) — A trader's own view of everything they have listed: grouping by state, filtering, empty states, and the actions each state allows. Read it when working on the listing management view.
- [todo.md](todo.md) — Backlog of listing capabilities, split into Phase 1 MVP musts and Phase 2 extension nice-to-haves. Read it when picking up listing work or deciding whether a capability belongs in the MVP.
